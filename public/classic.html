The problem

Sound familiar?

The 40-message thread

Someone posts "need Friday off" and the group explodes. Three people half-volunteer, two others change the subject, and two hours later nothing is resolved. You end up handling it manually anyway.

The app nobody opens

You pay for a scheduling tool, do the whole setup, and by week two everyone's back to texting you directly. The app just sits there. You can't blame them — it's one more thing to log into.

The midnight scramble

It's 11pm. Someone just cancelled their 7am. You're texting people one by one, half-apologizing for the hour, hoping someone answers before you give up and cover it yourself.

How it works

Set it up once. It handles the rest.

1

Two-minute setup

Add Relay to the Telegram group your team already uses. That's it. No new accounts for your staff.

2

Nothing changes for your team

Your staff keeps messaging the way they always have. Relay reads the conversation, tracks the schedule, and stays quiet until it's needed.

3

Bot does the chasing

When someone needs a shift covered, Relay reaches out to available people, collects responses, and tells you who's taking it — without you touching your phone.

4

A clean schedule, always up to date

Relay automatically keeps a simple, readable schedule for your whole team — managers and staff always know who's on, no spreadsheet maintenance required.

Your dashboard

A real dashboard — not a slideshow.

This is the live product. Watch it move through the screens your manager actually uses — schedule, staff, payroll, time clock — all updating automatically from your group chat.
